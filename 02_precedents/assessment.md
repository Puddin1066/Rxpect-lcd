# Acupuncture precedent — 2026-11-24
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/ncacal-decision-memo.aspx?NCAId=295
CMS evaluated 2014–2019 reviews/meta-analyses, patient pain/function, medications and quality of life, durability, generalizability and risks. Limited older-adult participation did not prevent coverage. Final NCD restricts nonspecific CLBP lasting >=12 weeks, 12 visits/90 days, 8 additional with improvement, 20 annual maximum, stop for no improvement. For OLP use analogous narrow eligibility and response-dependent continuation, not acupuncture's exact visit limits. Evidence base and benefit category differ; OLP's lack of objective function benefit and controlled long-term benefit are material weaknesses. NCD is not an LCD procedural comparator. Actual successful JK LCD precedent still needed. Do not assert equal evidence strength.
