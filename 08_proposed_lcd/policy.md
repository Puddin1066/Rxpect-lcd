## Controlling integrated objective (supersedes conflicting earlier wording)
Establish Medicare coverage for clinician-directed, remotely monitored, fully disclosed open-label placebo therapy for nonspecific chronic lower-back pain using existing RTM codes where applicable. One primary deliverable: a complete evidence-supported formal LCD request for this integrated clinical service. OLP efficacy, monitoring medical necessity, benefit category, coding and device eligibility are interdependent supporting requirements, not separate coverage campaigns. Do not seek separate payment for inert tablets or a standalone OLP LCD unless a documented controlling requirement necessitates it. LCD cannot override national rules. Commercial acceptance: providers can legitimately receive Medicare reimbursement for qualifying RxPect-associated RTM services; distinguish submission readiness, favorable coverage and actual compliant payment. Do not treat submission as commercial validation. Preserve independent repo boundaries and human release approval.

# Proposed policy language — draft for clinician and counsel review
Medicare coverage may be considered for clinician-directed, fully disclosed OLP adjunctive management of nonspecific chronic low-back pain of at least 12 weeks where clinical evaluation supports the diagnosis and a documented plan of care establishes medical necessity. OLP must not replace indicated diagnostic or therapeutic care. Patient understanding of the inactive intervention must be documented. Baseline pain/function and individualized goals must be recorded. Initial course proposed at 3–4 weeks, with reassessment and discontinuation or change if meaningful benefit absent. Associated RTM only when separately reasonable and necessary and all national requirements for service, device, practitioner and documentation are met. No separate payment requested for inert pills. Not a blanket coverage request for placebo, research services, or any particular software. Duration and response thresholds require clinical review and evidence justification. Evidence limitations in 03_clinical_evidence/review.md. No billing authorization.
