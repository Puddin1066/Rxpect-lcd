# Proposed LCD language for clinical and legal review

Medicare Part B coverage may be considered for clinician-directed musculoskeletal remote therapeutic monitoring furnished under an outpatient physical therapy plan of care for a beneficiary with chronic nonspecific low back pain who uses a fully disclosed investigational open-label placebo adjunct alongside usual PT, subject to applicable national requirements.

The record should establish the diagnosis, functional goals, and patient-specific reason for monitoring between visits. The plan should specify which adherence and response data will be collected, who reviews them, and how those data inform reassessment or an adjustment to the plan. At reassessment, document what the data showed, any clinical action taken, and the basis for continuation or discontinuation. Document patient disclosure and preserve access to indicated care.

Device supply, setup and education, and treatment management are payable only where each billed service meets applicable benefit, device, code, practitioner, documentation, data-day, time, and interactive communication requirements. The requested determination addresses monitoring only. The intervention itself is outside scope.

This draft depends on resolving whether monitoring the described adjunct is permitted under controlling Medicare policy and on evidence that the monitoring adds clinical value. See the [request draft](../09_submission/REQUEST_DRAFT.md) and [evidence review](../03_clinical_evidence/review.md).
