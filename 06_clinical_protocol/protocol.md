# Integrated OLP–RTM clinical protocol — proposed, 2026-11-25
Population: clinician-diagnosed nonspecific CLBP >=12 weeks; evaluate red flags and alternative diagnoses; continue indicated usual care. Obtain informed consent explaining inactive intervention, uncertain benefit and alternatives. No deception. 
Day 0: document baseline 0–10 pain score, Roland-Morris disability, individualized function goal, current care, and why remote monitoring is necessary for this patient rather than ordinary follow-up. Clinician selects treatment, provides instructions, assesses accessibility and device eligibility. 
Days 1–21: proposed daily patient self-report of adherence, pain and adverse events, with weekly functional assessment. Record actual data acquisition days, not merely logins. Missing data prompt a clinician-approved follow-up; deterioration triggers appropriate clinical assessment. 
Day 11: clinician reviews adherence and therapeutic response and discusses barriers, reinforces accurate instructions, or alters/stops plan. Day 21: reassess pain/function and patient preference; stop ineffective intervention; continuation requires individualized documented rationale and review. Proposed operational responder criterion >=30% pain reduction with no meaningful functional deterioration, subject to clinical validation, not a CMS requirement. No automatic continuation or claim.
Evidence basis: Carvalho used a three-week oral OLP course with interim day-11 clinician contact [1]. Kleine-Borgmann showed subjective benefit but no objective mobility benefit [2]. Neither study establishes incremental benefit of remote monitoring over ordinary follow-up. Daily remote collection is a proposed translation, not an established equivalence. Clinician assessment of actual actionability and burden required.
RTM claim gate: actual qualifying device, musculoskeletal monitoring, eligible clinician, qualifying data days, documented management time and real-time interaction where required; no duplicate time; no claim merely for issuing pills or automated reminders. CMS 2026 update distinguishes 2–15 and 16–30 day supply tiers [3,4]. Verify applicable year and full rules before billing.
