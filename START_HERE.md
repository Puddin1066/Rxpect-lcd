## Controlling objective

Develop a Jurisdiction K LCD request for Medicare Part B reimbursement of medically necessary musculoskeletal remote therapeutic monitoring (RTM) furnished under an outpatient physical therapy plan of care for nonspecific chronic low back pain. The patient uses a fully disclosed investigational open-label placebo (OLP) adjunct alongside usual PT. The requested coverage is for monitoring device supply and clinician services under applicable RTM codes. The OLP intervention and any future digital therapeutic payment pathway fall outside the requested determination. The LCD must conform to national coverage, coding and device requirements.
