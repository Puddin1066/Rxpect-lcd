## Evidence and precedent analysis
CMS acupuncture decision memo CAG-00452N (2020): https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/ncacal-decision-memo.aspx?NCAId=295
CMS examined systematic reviews from 2014–2019; outcomes included pain, function, medication use, quality of life. Medicare-age enrollment was limited. CMS considered durability, patient-relevant outcomes, benefit-risk, generalizability and the opioid public health context. The proposed memo's cited review characterized short-term functional and pain improvements as slight, with low certainty for several outcomes and no clear intermediate functional benefit. This is evidence that modest effects and imperfect geriatric data do not categorically prevent coverage, not evidence that OLP satisfies the same threshold. Acupuncture has a larger evidence base and distinct statutory and service considerations. NCD is not an LCD precedent procedurally. Source: https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/ncacal-decision-memo.aspx?ncaid=295&proposed=Y

OLP CLBP meta-analysis: Flavio-Reis et al., 2025, PMID 39962721, PMCID PMC11881878, DOI 10.1080/17581869.2025.2467025. Four studies, 171 OLP and 161 control; pooled NRS mean difference -0.62 (95% CI -1.09 to -0.14), I2 31%, very low GRADE certainty. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39962721/ ; full text locator https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11881878/ . Do not claim clinically meaningful average change or opioid savings from this result alone. Prespecify responder-level pain and function thresholds, and assess durability.
Comparator gap matrix: pain signal present/modest; functional benefit needs verified extraction; durability insufficiently characterized; age applicability unresolved; safety systematically appraise; incremental RTM efficacy not demonstrated; benefit category and device legality unresolved.

## Proposed evidence review protocol
Search PubMed, Cochrane and trial registries for (open label placebo OR open-label placebo OR disclosed placebo) AND (low back pain OR musculoskeletal pain); separate expectancy interventions without OLP. Record search date, exact strings, deduplication, exclusions; extract allocation, comparator, population age, outcome scale, time point, effect/CI, adverse events, attrition, funding. Assess RoB2 and GRADE; avoid double counting trial populations in pooled syntheses. Separate evidence for intervention from evidence supporting RTM. Include null results and long-term follow-up. Independent reviewer signoff required.
