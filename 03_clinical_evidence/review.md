# Preliminary evidence appraisal — web search 2026-11-24
Queries: open label placebo chronic low back pain randomized trial systematic review 2025 2026 Carvalho Kleine Borgmann long term follow up; open label placebo chronic back pain randomized trial Ashar 2024 injection Ikemoto 2020 Kleine Borgmann 3 year follow up. Target population nonspecific CLBP; oral OLP adjunct vs usual care; injection indirect; outcomes pain, function, duration, harms, Medicare age. Targeted search only, not exhaustive systematic review.
| Study | Finding | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Kleine-Borgmann 2019, PMID 31479068 | 122 randomized; 3-week pain d=-0.44, subjective disability d=-0.45 | No objective mobility benefit |
| Kleine-Borgmann 3-year follow-up PMID 35947884 | 89 participants; no between-group difference in any outcome | Attrition; no support for durable 3-week course |
| Carvalho 5-year follow-up PMID 33259459 | 55 participants; improvements persisted | Uncontrolled; cannot attribute persistence to OLP |
| Ashar 2024 PMID 39259542 | Injection OLP pain benefit at primary endpoint; not significant across full follow-up | Different delivery modality; age 21–70 |
| Flavio-Reis 2025 PMID 39962721 | 332 participants, pooled NRS MD -0.62 (95% CI -1.09,-0.14), I2 31% | Very low certainty |
| Borg 2025 PMID 40617928 | 703 participants across 7 MSK RCTs; pain SMD 0.46 (0.28–0.63), subjective function 0.40 (0.19–0.60) | Low certainty PROMs; moderate certainty no objective function effect |
